How the ratings are made: these are Claude's estimates, not official numbers. Player order follows Hotspawn's Worlds 2026 role power rankings (four editors each). Ratings are then adjusted with Strafe's top-5 lists and 2026 stats (KDA, kill participation, gold/XP at 15, DPM, solo kills, deaths). Stats from the LCS, LCP and CBLOL are discounted for league strength. Top Esports' 369 replaces ZUIAN (visa) and has only a handful of 2026 games.

Favourite picks: each player's three most-played champions in 2026 pro play (games, win rate) from mid.gg player pages. LOS players come from their Sheep Esports player pages, with CBLOL playoff stats from Sheep's event stats.

Meta status: pick + ban presence in Worlds 2026 games on patch 26.20 (play-in, 22 games) from NerfPlz. Ryze is counted as meta because he is the most-picked champion across the latest pro games on NerfPlz's pro-meta page. The sample is small, so treat "Not seen" as "untested at Worlds", not "bad".
