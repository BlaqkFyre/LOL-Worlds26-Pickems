Worlds 2026 · Patch 26.20 · Pick'Em scouting

How the ratings are made: these are Claude's estimates, not official numbers. Player order follows Hotspawn's Worlds 2026 role power rankings (four editors each). Ratings are then adjusted with Strafe's top-5 lists and 2026 stats (KDA, kill participation, gold/XP at 15, DPM, solo kills, deaths). Stats from the LCS, LCP and CBLOL are discounted for league strength. Top Esports' 369 replaces ZUIAN (visa) and has only a handful of 2026 games.

Favourite picks: each player's three most-played champions in 2026 pro play (games, win rate) from mid.gg player pages. LOS players come from their Sheep Esports player pages, with CBLOL playoff stats from Sheep's event stats.

Meta status and champion cards: blended pick + ban presence from NerfPlz pro tables — the Worlds patch 26.20 (25 games) weighted ×4 with patches 26.16–26.20 (465 games) for 90%, plus the 2026 season (2,723 games) for 10%. Win rates are shrunk toward 50% for small samples. Builds, roles and play-in users come from NerfPlz champion pages (Leaguepedia data). Champion cards and player cards show this tournament's own numbers first; the pro research drives draft suggestions, the AI and the simulation.

Game odds: the simulator is rule-based with random rolls. Its win odds were checked against 711 real 2026 pro series (LCK, LPL, LEC, LCS, LCP, CBLOL, First Stand, MSI, EWC, Worlds play-in) and recalibrated, because it had been far too confident. Those results are used only for that test. Details: research/backtest-2026.md in the GitHub repo.

Real Worlds games: the Swiss games played so far (picks, bans, player stats and objectives, from NerfPlz, gol.gg and dpm.lol) feed the champion cards' Worlds meta, pairs and counters, players' champion pools, draft suggestions, and a small capped "Worlds form" update to player skills (at most ±1 per game and ±3 in total, compared with the average for that role at Worlds). Simulated tournaments still start at 0-0; real results never set simulated standings.

Region badges: the coloured badge before a team or player shows its league seed (e.g. #1) in its region's colour; hover it for the region. Colours: ■ LCK, ■ LPL, ■ LEC, ■ LCS, ■ LCP, ■ CBLOL.

Images: champion square icons load from Riot's Data Dragon. Teams are shown as colour badges only — no team or Riot logos.

Attributes: Top — Laning, Teamfight, Consistency · Jungle — Early game, Objectives, Teamfight · Mid — Laning, Roaming, Damage · ADC — Laning, Damage, Positioning · Support — Playmaking, Map presence, Reliability.
