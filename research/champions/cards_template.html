Champion Scouting Cards

Every League champion with Riot's own class, subclass, damage type and 1–3 style ratings, plus where pros play it at Worlds 2026 (patch 16.20) and across the 2026 season. Built from the champion database in the Pick'Em Lab repo.

Sources: Riot client data from the League wiki's champion data module (read 14 Nov 2026); pro play from the Oracle's Elixir 2026 file (major leagues; Worlds = 94 games). Meta role = most-played pro role at Worlds with 5+ games, else across 2026 majors with 5+, else Riot's first lane. Off-roles = other roles with 10%+ and 2+ games. Ratings are Riot's 1–3 client ratings (damage, toughness, crowd control, mobility, utility). Damage index = the champion's average share of team damage in its meta role over that role's average. No Riot artwork is used.
